A quick briefing

How to play

  1. Choose Basic or Hard mode. Hard questions award more points.
  2. Solve the math card. Pick the one correct answer from the four cards.
  3. Unlock a letter. Each correct answer reveals exactly one letter, in a scrambled position—not left to right.
  4. Use the clue. Letters and category clues help you identify the mystery word.
  5. Guess whenever you want. You can enter a word before any letters are revealed. A wrong guess costs points, but the game continues.
  6. Keep solving. After each correct answer, a new randomly generated question appears. Mystery words do not repeat until the collection has been used.

Answer cards use number values; decimals are shown to at most two places.
